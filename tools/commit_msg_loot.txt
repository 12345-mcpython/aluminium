content+engine: 1506's loot box picks one of three - and the two spellings it needed to be writable

The three-way pick ships now. It needed three things that did not exist, and all three are in:

  * `one_of` (previous commit) - a rule executes ONE of its own effects;
  * the battle's SKILL POINTS become reachable as 「战技点」. Measured, nothing anywhere had ever gained or spent them,
    because they live in `Battle.skillPointPolicy` rather than in a ResourceManager. GAIN_RESOURCE now routes that name to
    the policy, and `requireReadableResources` exempts it: no character can honestly declare a counter the battle owns;
  * `highest_hp_enemy` - "the enemy target with the highest HP", over ALL opponents. The trigger is a spent Skill Point,
    which carries no hit set, so `highest_hp_attack_hit` (the ATTACK-HIT pool) could not answer it. It is the same pair of
    pools `random_enemy` and `random_hit_enemy` already distinguish.

The judge reads a COUNT over 18 seeds, and it took two measurement mistakes to get there - both worth recording:

  * "the enemy's HP moved" is NOT a marker for the pick: the event carries TWO rules, the constant 90% damage and the
    pick, so HP moves either way. The rider is therefore stated large (500% of ATK) so that its landing is unmistakable
    beside that constant;
  * "skill points went up" is not one either, for the same reason - so the readings are taken either side of the ONE event,
    and a pick counts as the skill-point branch only when the rider did not also land.

Over 18 seeds the three branches all appear: 笑点 +3, 战技点 +2, and the 2086-damage rider.

⚠ Registered, not approximated: 【超超超大剑】's basis is 「本次总伤害值 20%」, and the rider is written as 20% of her
ATTACK. The engine has `original_damage`, but its basis is the settled instance of THIS trigger - a thing to measure
before it is a thing to write.
