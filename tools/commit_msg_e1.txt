content: Eidolon 1's retained fifth - the two rules were one rule's worth of work

「退出【无敌玩家】状态时不会清空【隐藏分】，改为保留 20% 的【隐藏分】。」

This was recorded as OPEN with a measured 0 where a fifth of her 隐藏分 belongs, and the cause is not the spelling of the
share: `previousCredited` - the quantity `amount_from_previous` reads - is per-RULE. `TriggerInterpreter.apply` starts it
at 0 and walks ONE rule's effects, so a retention written as its OWN rule cannot see what the clearing rule took, and the
share is 20% of nothing.

Merged into one rule, with the Eidolon gate on the EFFECT rather than on the rule (a rule-level gate would remove the
base clear, which must always happen):

    SPEND_RESOURCE  spendAll                    - take everything
    GAIN_RESOURCE   amountFromPrevious  0.2    - restore a fifth of what that actually took

Measured: she holds 80 at the exit of 【无敌玩家】 (the Ultimate and its trace pay 20 before the fixture's own 60), and the
rule now keeps 16. At E0 the base clear still leaves 0.

The judge's own expectation was wrong first: it wanted a fifth of 60 and read the rule as broken. Corrected against the
measured holding, and the case that used to be `_OPEN` is a real one again.
